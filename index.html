Learnnest is a specialist early intervention practice for children aged 3 to 8 — working with children with learning differences before the gap widens.

Reach out to us →

Every child learns. Not every child learns the same way.

Some children are bright, curious and full of ideas — but find reading, writing or making sense of numbers harder than it should be. They are not behind. They are not less. They are children whose learning needs a different approach — and the earlier that approach begins, the better the outcome.

That is what Learnnest is for.

Specialist early intervention —
on your child's own campus.

Who we work with

Children aged 3 to 8 showing early signs of learning differences — children who would benefit from structured early intervention before those signs become wider gaps.

How we work

Small specialist-led groups. Hands-on, activity-based sessions designed around each child's learning profile. Every session observed, documented and built upon.

Where we work

On school campuses — after school hours. Currently at Koorooma Foundation Years, Vajrahalli, Bengaluru. Open to all families in the community.

Every child enters at their own level.
Every child moves at their own pace.

Stage 1 · Ages 3–4

Language and Play

Building oral language, expressive communication and early listening skills through structured play and story. The foundation everything else grows from.

Stage 2 · Ages 4–6

Early Foundations

Strengthening attention, fine motor skills, sequencing and early literacy and numeracy readiness through hands-on activity. Specialist observation in every session.

Stage 3 · Ages 6–8

Learning Support

Structured early intervention for children with identified learning differences. Small groups of 4 to 5 children. Specialist-led every session. Consistent, documented, parent-partnered.

No child is labelled here. Every child is seen.

Children with learning differences — dyslexia, dyscalculia, dysgraphia — experience the world richly and think in ways that standard classrooms are not always built for. Early intervention does not change who they are. It builds the skills and confidence that let who they are come forward.

Two specialist educators.
One shared conviction.

Learnnest was founded by two special educators who have spent years working with children and young adults with learning differences across institutions. What brought us here — to early intervention, to the earliest years — is the same thing: the belief that the right support at the right time changes the entire trajectory of a child's learning life.
