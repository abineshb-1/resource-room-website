Our Story

Two educators.
Twenty-five years of experience.
One shared conviction.

Early intervention changes the trajectory of a child's learning life. We have seen what happens when it comes too late. Learnnest exists so it comes early.

How we got here.

Both of us began our careers as engineers. Both of us left engineering for the same reason — children.

We found our way into classrooms separately, through different doors. One through Montessori — drawn to the philosophy that every child carries their own way of learning and the educator's job is to find it. The other through special education — drawn to the children the system most often misses, and the families navigating that journey with very little guidance.

For years we worked in schools — designing curricula, training teachers, building individualised plans for children, sitting with parents who were confused and quietly worried. We worked with children across a wide range of learning needs. And we worked, for a significant part of our careers, with older children and young adults who were trying to catch up — students who had reached their teens still struggling with the foundations that should have been built at 4, 5, or 6.

That pattern is what brought us here. Not to older learners. To the earliest years. To the window that matters most — before the gap widens, before the child builds a story about themselves as someone who cannot learn, before the family runs out of options.

Learnnest is the practice we wish had existed for the children we have spent our careers working with. It exists now — for the children who are 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 today.

What guides everything we do.

Every child is already capable.

The work is not to fix what is missing — it is to find what is already there and build from it.

The earliest years are the most important.

What happens between 3 and 8 shapes everything that comes after. That window deserves the most careful attention.

A different way of learning is not a deficit.

A child who finds reading, writing or numbers harder than expected is not a struggling child. They are a child whose learning needs a different approach — and finding that approach changes everything.

Families are part of the work.

A child does not exist only in a session. The parents who know their child best are our most important partners — always.

The people behind Learnnest.

Thilagavathy Abinesh

Co-Founder · Montessori Educator · SLD Specialist

Thilagavathy began her career as an engineer — and found her way to education through a growing conviction that the most important work happens in the earliest years of a child's life.

Over fifteen years she trained and practised as a Montessori educator at both Primary and Elementary levels — building a deep understanding of how young children learn, how environments shape attention and confidence, and how individualised learning plans change outcomes for children who need something different from the standard classroom.

In recent years she completed specialist training in Specific Learning Differences — bringing a clinical awareness to her Montessori-informed practice that very few educators in this space combine. She currently works directly with children with learning differences every day — in a specialist school in the mornings, and through Learnnest in the evenings.

Her founding question — the one that drives everything at Learnnest — is simple: what would have been possible for these children if the right support had come at 4 instead of 14?

Photo

Dhivya Nagarajan

Co-Founder · Special Educator · Inclusion Specialist

Dhivya has spent a decade working at the intersection of special education and inclusion — across Montessori, CBSE and IB frameworks — meeting children with diverse learning needs exactly where they are.

Her expertise spans learning disabilities, autism spectrum, ADHD and intellectual and developmental disabilities. She designs individualised education programmes, builds differentiated assessments, and works with sensory and behavioural approaches that honour each child's unique pace and profile. She is currently completing her B.Ed in Special Education and working toward RCI certification.

But beyond her professional experience, Dhivya brings something no qualification can give — the lived experience of being a mother to a child with special needs. She knows what it feels like to sit across from a specialist and wonder if your child is truly being seen. She knows the exhaustion of navigating systems that were not built for your child. And she knows the difference a knowledgeable, warm, unhurried specialist can make to a family carrying that weight.

She doesn't just teach. She walks alongside families — with expertise and with understanding that comes from having been there herself.

What we bring together.

Between us we carry fifteen years of Montessori practice, ten years of special education, SLD specialist training, IEP design, sensory and behavioural intervention, and curriculum development across three educational frameworks — and the lived experience of what it means to parent a child who learns differently.

We have worked with children across a wide range of learning needs and ages. We have seen — again and again — what happens when a child with a learning difference reaches their teens without the early support they needed. The gap that could have been closed at 5 becomes the story they carry at 15.

At Learnnest we bring all of that experience into one small, calm, specialist space — built specifically for children aged 3 to 8 — at the moment when early intervention makes the greatest difference.
